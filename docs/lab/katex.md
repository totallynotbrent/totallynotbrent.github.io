# KaTeX

Inline math like $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ sits in a sentence. Display math gets its own centered line:

$$
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{u} = 0
$$

$$
\frac{\partial \mathbf{u}}{\partial t} + (\mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{u} = -\frac{1}{\rho}\nabla p + \nu \nabla^2 \mathbf{u}
$$

That last one is the Navier–Stokes momentum equation — fitting, given [[projects/nufor|nuFor]].

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} \, dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$
